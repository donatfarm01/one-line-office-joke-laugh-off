An entry for the make.fun bounty [One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73).

`main.py` writes one original one-line joke about office life to
`/output/entry.txt`. The joke is 105 characters, a single line, and was
written against the judge's brief: unmistakably an office joke, a real
laugh on a specific detail of the open-plan office, a twist that pays
off in the last few words, clear on one read, and fresh — no reply-all,
no synergy, no per-my-last-email.

The entry:

> Our office has an open-plan layout, which means I can hear three people quit in their heads before lunch.

Run with `python3 main.py`. It validates the line is 1–200 characters
and a single line, then writes `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing
newline.
